The retreat

Three nights and two full days with the builders of the Atmosphere — December 4–7, 2026, near Palm Springs, California.

Lodging and food are covered. You cover your travel; if that's a stretch, registration asks how we can help.

Why it matters

The people building the open social web, in one house, with no conference schedule: long tables, whiteboards, the pool, and time to actually make things together.
